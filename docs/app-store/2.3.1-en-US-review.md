# Jotley 2.3.1 — English App Store copy for review

## Description

Get free, unlimited live transcription and summaries with Jotley. See your words appear as you speak, then use Apple Intelligence to summarize your transcript on your device.

Jotley is built on Apple Intelligence and works on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Turn meetings, lectures and quick thoughts into searchable notes you can edit, organize and share. No subscription or account is required for transcription and summarization.

TURN TALK INTO NOTES

Apple’s on-device speech recognition turns your words into text as you speak. Pause when you need to, then continue in the same note. Timestamps make it easier to find the part you want. Edit transcripts and summaries directly, use Find and Replace to fix repeated mistakes, and add names or specialized terms to your custom word list.

GET THE KEY POINTS

Apple Intelligence turns your transcript into a summary with key points and action items. Let Jotley choose a format, pick a built-in template or create your own. Ask a question about your note and Apple Intelligence uses the note to find an answer, surface an action item or clarify a detail.

FIND AND SHARE YOUR NOTES

Search your notes, organize them with folders and tags, or let Jotley suggest titles and tags and file notes automatically. Export notes as text or Markdown, share a summary as a PDF, and optionally sync your library with iCloud. On Mac, you can transcribe microphone and system audio and export to Obsidian.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Transcription, summaries and answers run on your device. Jotley saves the transcript without saving an audio file. Once the Apple models are ready, you can transcribe, summarize and ask questions about your notes offline. Tips and personal technical support are optional paid purchases.

Jotley’s interface is available in 16 languages. Transcription languages depend on the Apple speech models available for your device. Summaries and answers require a compatible device with Apple Intelligence enabled.

## What's New

- The Jotley interface is now available in 16 languages.
- Automatic titles and filing work better on short notes. Notes without a clear folder match can go to Misc.
- Find and Replace now works in summaries as well as transcripts.
- On iPhone and iPad, scroll to reveal a shortcut to the top or bottom of a summary or transcript.
- Choose whether to receive summary notifications during setup. The option starts on and uses your system notification permission.
- Refined note controls and improved the personal support ticket experience.
