# Jotley onboarding artwork

Jotley uses flat editorial illustrations with faceted shapes, broad details, and transparent backgrounds. The palette pairs violet (#7C3AED) with lilac (#E9DDFF), ink violet (#2A1B46), and white. The illustrations share a simple vocabulary of sound bars, note sheets, and angled corners. The welcome, intelligence, language, microphone, computer audio, and support scenes use the same artwork on Mac, iPhone, and iPad wherever those steps appear.

The artwork was generated with ImageGen from the creative director’s scene directions. The language globe is a hand-drawn vector kept in `docs/design/onboarding-source/`; render the SVG to a 1280 × 1280 transparent PNG to update it. The globe keeps its latitude lines so it reads as a globe. The microphone keeps its original ImageGen art: a violet capsule with two white horizontal stripes and two sound marks. Each asset is centered on a 1280 × 1280 transparent canvas and lives at `onboarding-*.imageset/illustration.png` in both asset catalogs. The onboarding view supplies its layout and accessible labels.

The Jotley brand mark is a pure white quill on solid #7C3AED in a rounded rectangle. Use the same geometry for light and dark appearances. The app icon has a square source canvas so the operating system can apply its rounded-rectangle mask.
